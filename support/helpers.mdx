## Your main responsibility

Monitor **❓┃questions** and encourage players with questions to use that forum. Management's goal is to make questions and answers easier to find.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the forum">
    Look for new questions and posts waiting for a response when you are available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give an honest answer">
    Answer to the best of your ability. If you are unsure, say so and ask someone who can confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Direct players to the forum">
    Encourage players asking questions elsewhere to post in **❓┃questions**. Where useful, link the relevant [player guide](https://fleetempire.hxl.au/docs).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Response expectations

Management encourages quick responses, but does not expect excellence or around-the-clock attention. Everyone has a life outside Fleet Empire.

Your work in the questions forum contributes to your Helper performance assessment and helps management assess readiness for progression.

## When you need help

For questions about your responsibilities, contact management, **@magiccarc** or **@Ace** directly.
