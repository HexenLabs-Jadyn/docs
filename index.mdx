## The essentials

- **Every punishment must be logged** in the appropriate log channel.
- **Permanent bans require Community Manager+ approval.**
- **Changes to the punishment guidelines require Administrator+ approval.**
- Use Discord's native timeout, kick and ban controls. FleetMod prompts you to record the action.
- If FleetMod is offline, use manual logs.

<Warning>
  A promotion does not automatically grant every moderation permission. Work within your assigned role and ask management when your authority is unclear.
</Warning>

## Supporting players

Helpers monitor **❓┃questions** and direct players with questions into that forum. Answer honestly and to the best of your ability. If you do not know the answer, ask for help rather than guess.
