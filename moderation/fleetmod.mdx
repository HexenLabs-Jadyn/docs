FleetMod works with Discord's native moderation controls. Use **Timeout**, **Kick** or **Ban** in Discord; the bot then sends a prompt for you to log the action. You do not need slash commands for this workflow.

<Warning>
  Every punishment must be logged. You have **one hour** to complete a FleetMod log before management is notified.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the correct action">
    Check the [punishment guidelines](/moderation/punishments), your assigned permissions and any [approval requirements](/moderation/approvals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Moderate through Discord">
    Use Discord's native timeout, kick or ban control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the bot prompt">
    Provide a reason. You may also attach a screenshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the log succeeded">
    Make sure the log was submitted successfully in the appropriate log channel within **one hour**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If FleetMod is offline

You are required to use **manual logs** in the appropriate log channel. The logging requirement still applies.

## Missed logs and staff warnings

If you do not log successfully, you receive a **staff warning**. Management states that **three staff warnings result in removal**.

These staff warnings concern logging compliance. They are separate from the warnings listed in the player punishment guidelines.

## Escalation prompts

After **three timeouts**, FleetMod asks whether you want to kick the user. The announcement also describes a similar prompt for bans. These prompts are discretionary; they do not replace the punishment guidelines or required approvals.

<Note>
  The **15-day reset applies to escalation history** for timeouts and bans, resetting the three-exclusion limit. It does not automatically end active punishments or unban users.
</Note>

## Bugs and oversight

FleetMod is new. Report bugs directly to the bot's maintainer. Moderation actions are heavily logged; do not attempt to bypass the logging system.
