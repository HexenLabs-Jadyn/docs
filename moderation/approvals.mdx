## Required approvals

| Action | Approval required |
| --- | --- |
| Modify a punishment from the prescribed guidelines | **Administrator+** |
| Issue a permanent ban | **Community Manager+** |
| Log any punishment | Required for **every punishment** |

<Warning>
  Obtain approval before issuing a permanent ban, including cases where the guidelines prescribe a permanent, unappealable ban.
</Warning>

## How the requirements work together

If a proposed change creates a permanent ban, the permanent-ban approval requirement still applies. Administrator+ approval to modify a punishment does not replace Community Manager+ approval for a permanent ban.

## When the guidance is unclear

Ask the appropriate approving rank before taking an action outside the documented guidance. For questions about your role, contact management, **@magiccarc** or **@Ace**.

## After approval

Carry out the approved action within your assigned permissions and log it in the appropriate channel. FleetMod's one-hour logging deadline still applies to its Discord moderation workflow.
