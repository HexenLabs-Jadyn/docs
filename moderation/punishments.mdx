Use the sequence for the relevant offence. **Every punishment must be logged.** Changes require **Administrator+ approval**, and permanent bans require **Community Manager+ approval**.

<Warning>
  Do not issue a permanent ban without Community Manager+ approval, even when a permanent ban is prescribed below.
</Warning>

## Quick reference

| Offence | Prescribed sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Cheating or exploiting | Permanent, unappealable game and Discord ban |
| Disrespect | 1-day chat mute → server kick → appealable ban |
| Inappropriate behaviour | 1-day chat mute → server kick → appealable ban → permanent, unappealable game and Discord ban |
| Spamming | Warning → 1-day chat mute → server kick → appealable Discord ban and permanent in-game chat mute |
| Rank impersonation | Warning → 1-day chat mute → appealable Discord ban and permanent in-game chat mute |
| Doxxing | Permanent, unappealable game and Discord ban |
| Blackmailing | Permanent, unappealable game and Discord ban |

## Moderator discretion

Appealable ban durations are at moderator discretion. For disrespect and inappropriate behaviour, moderators also decide whether an appealable ban applies in-game, on Discord or both. Follow the prescribed escalation sequence and obtain any required approvals.

## Offence details

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Cheating or exploiting">
    Cheating or exploiting means doing something outside the game's natural or intended gameplay.

    **Punishment:** Permanent, unappealable game and Discord ban.

    **Approval:** Community Manager+ before issuing the permanent ban.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Disrespect">
    Active defamation or discouragement of any player, regardless of rank. Examples include hate speech, aggressive arguments and mass shouting in-game.

    1. **1-day chat mute** in-game and on Discord, regardless of where the offence occurred.
    2. **Server kick** from the game or Discord, depending on where it occurred.
    3. **Appealable ban.**

    The **duration and platform scope of the appealable ban are at moderator discretion**. Permanent bans still require Community Manager+ approval.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Inappropriate behaviour">
    Gruesome, violent, sexual or otherwise unprofessional conduct that violates community standards, including conduct that compromises other players' safety or well-being.

    1. **1-day chat mute** in-game and on Discord, regardless of where the offence occurred.
    2. **Server kick** from the platform where the inappropriate behaviour occurred.
    3. **Appealable ban.**
    4. **Permanent, unappealable game and Discord ban.**

    The **duration and platform scope of the appealable ban are at moderator discretion**. The permanent game and Discord ban requires Community Manager+ approval.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Spamming">
    Repeated identical messages intended to annoy or disrupt others.

    1. **Warning** in-game and on Discord, regardless of where the offence occurred.
    2. **1-day chat mute** in-game and on Discord, regardless of where it occurred.
    3. **Server kick** from the game or Discord, depending on where it occurred.
    4. **Appealable Discord ban** and **permanent in-game chat mute.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rank impersonation">
    Falsely claiming a staff position, official title or elevated role you do not hold. Examples include misleading usernames, unauthorised rank tags or claiming authority and permissions you do not have.

    1. **Warning** in-game and on Discord, regardless of where the offence occurred.
    2. **1-day chat mute** in-game and on Discord, regardless of where it occurred.
    3. **Appealable Discord ban** and **permanent in-game chat mute.**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Doxxing">
    Unauthorised gathering, sharing or publishing of another person's private or personally identifiable information. Examples include real names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, social profiles or photographs disclosed without consent.

    **Punishment:** Permanent, unappealable game and Discord ban.

    **Approval:** Community Manager+ before issuing the permanent ban.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Blackmailing">
    Threatening to reveal private information, report a false infraction or harm another player. This includes demanding currency, items, favours or real-life compensation in exchange for withholding damaging information or refraining from hostile actions.

    **Punishment:** Permanent, unappealable game and Discord ban.

    **Approval:** Community Manager+ before issuing the permanent ban.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
