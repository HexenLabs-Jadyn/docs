Before you start helping players or moderating, review these essentials.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand your role">
    Read [helper duties](/support/helpers) and [roles and progression](/staff/roles). Confirm the permissions actually assigned to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know where questions belong">
    Monitor **❓┃questions**. Encourage players to post there so answers are easier to find.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review punishment guidelines">
    Use the [punishment reference](/moderation/punishments). Obtain required approval before issuing a permanent ban or changing a prescribed punishment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know how to log">
    FleetMod records native Discord moderation and prompts you for a reason. Complete its log within **one hour**. If the bot is offline, use manual logs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask when unsure">
    Contact management, **@magiccarc** or **@Ace** for role questions. Report FleetMod bugs directly to the bot's maintainer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A practical routine

1. Check the questions forum for unanswered posts.
2. Respond clearly and honestly.
3. Direct questions from other channels to the forum.
4. Review any outstanding moderation logs.
5. Watch for training announcements and scheduling polls.

<Tip>
  Management does not expect around-the-clock availability. When you are available, aim for timely, professional responses.
</Tip>
