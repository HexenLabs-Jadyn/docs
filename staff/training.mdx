## Training

Management plans training to explain your role and the expectations placed on Helpers. Watch staff announcements for a scheduling poll and confirmed dates.

The original announcement said a poll would be posted within 24 hours. That was an announcement-relative timeframe; this handbook does not establish a new deadline or confirm that training is scheduled.

## What management expects

- Answer player questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Monitor **❓┃questions** when available.
- Encourage players to use the forum for questions.
- Aim for quick responses without assuming around-the-clock availability.
- Stay professional.
- Log every punishment and respect approval requirements.

Contact management, **@magiccarc** or **@Ace** with questions about your role or expectations.
