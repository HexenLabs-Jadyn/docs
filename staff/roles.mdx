## Helpers

Helpers support players, monitor **❓┃questions** and direct questions into that forum. Management considers activity and professionalism when assessing readiness for Trial Moderator.

<Card title="Helper duties" icon="comments" href="/support/helpers">
  Read the forum workflow and response expectations.
</Card>

## Trial Moderators

Management describes Trial Moderators as able to:

- Handle **test tickets**.
- **Do not handle refund or report tickets**; pass these to staff authorised to handle them.
- Issue **timeouts**.
- Use assigned **in-game permissions**.

Use only the in-game permissions assigned to you. Confirm unfamiliar permissions with management before using them.

## Progression guidance

| Stage | Management's stated guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Helper → Trial Moderator | Helpers stay in the role for around **one week** before some may progress. Activity and professionalism matter. |
| Trial Moderator → next promotion | Management mentions roughly **2–4 weeks** in the trial role. |

<Note>
  These are approximate assessment periods, not guaranteed promotion dates. Management states that promotions will not be made hastily.
</Note>

## Performance

Questions-forum participation partly contributes to Helper performance assessment. Aim for accurate answers, timely responses when available and professional conduct.

Permissions depend on your assigned role. Promotion guidance does not authorise actions beyond those permissions.
